# One Dream Architecture

The public website of One Dream Architecture, an architecture practice: its portfolio, and the paid webinars it runs.

## Language

**Firm**:
One Dream Architecture, the practice the site represents. The site is under the practice's name, not any individual's.
_Avoid_: Company, studio, client

**Webinar**:
A scheduled live online session run by the Firm, with its own page, that people book a seat on.
_Avoid_: Event, workshop, session

**Registrant**:
A person who has paid for and booked a seat on a Webinar.
_Avoid_: Attendee (until they join), customer, user, lead

**Attendee**:
A Registrant who actually joined the live Webinar.
_Avoid_: Participant, viewer
